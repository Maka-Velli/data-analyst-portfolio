What the dashboard says

  • The top three emitters — China, the United States and India — produce 54.7% of global CO2; the top ten produce 71.1%.
  • The 23 growth-engine countries produce 61.8% of global CO2 and added 4,761 Mt since 2013. The 18 managed decliners cut 1,992 Mt over the same period.
  • Coal is the dominant fuel in the priority countries: 71.1% of China's CO2, 66.6% of India's, 81.9% of South Africa's and 58.1% of Vietnam's.
  • Per-capita emissions range from 0.1 t in the poorest countries to 32.8 t in Qatar; the global median is 3.2 t per person.
  • A 20% reduction in the growth engines' 2013–2023 trajectory would avoid roughly 950 Mt of CO2 per year by 2033.
Source: Our World in Data — CO2 and Greenhouse Gas Emissions data. Priority score = 40% absolute emissions + 30% emissions growth 2013–2023 + 20% carbon intensity (2022) + 10% coal share, ranked among the 41 countries emitting at least 100 Mt in 2023.
